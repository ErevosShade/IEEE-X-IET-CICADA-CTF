The FRONTIER track problem statements for the **screening round** are now live. Teams who have completed registration can access them via the [FRONTIER statements page](/tracks/frontier/statements).

You have until **28 November 2026 at 23:59 IST** to submit your screening solution.

### What to Submit

- A 2-page solution brief (PDF)
- A 3-minute demo video (Unlisted YouTube link)
- Your GitHub repository (must be public)

Evaluation will be done by the FRONTIER jury within 7 days of the deadline. Shortlisted teams will be notified by 5 December.

Good luck. The problem space is deliberately open-ended — we want to see how you think, not just what you build.
