You can join **Open Innovation**, **Research Track**, or both — even if you are already in a CORE or FRONTIER team. These entries are completely independent: joining an add-on will not alter your primary team, mode, or screening submission.

### How to Take Part:

1. Sign in and navigate to **Dashboard → Open Innovation & Research**.
2. Choose **Enter Open Innovation** or **Enter Research Track**.
3. Select your category, verify your institution details and ID, and submit your entry.
4. Participate solo or collaborate in a dedicated add-on team of up to four members.

Every team member must submit their own entry confirmation before joining an add-on squad.
