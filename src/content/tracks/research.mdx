## Research Track

Explore fundamental scientific questions, submit novel architectures, or showcase ongoing research work.

Whether you are investigating quantum information models, resilient distributed consensus, neuromorphic algorithms, or novel composite materials — the CICADE Research Track connects student researchers with distinguished faculty panels and IEEE/IET fellowship opportunities.

### Highlights:

- **Guided Problem Statements** available for students entering academic research for the first time.
- **Faculty Jury & Mentorship** providing feedback on experimental methodology and write-ups.
- **Publication & Fellowship Opportunities** supported by university labs.
