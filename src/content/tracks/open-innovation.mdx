## Open Innovation Track

Bring an original idea, early-stage venture, or independent build to the CICADE stage. 

Newcomers can tackle a domain they are passionate about with dedicated mentorship support, while student founders and teams with existing pitch decks or early prototypes can refine and pitch directly to investors and industry leaders.

### Highlights:

- **Founder & Startup Mentorship** with seasoned product leads and entrepreneurs.
- **Investor & Incubation Fast-tracks** through partner angel networks.
- Independent add-on: can be pursued simultaneously alongside CORE or FRONTIER tracks.
