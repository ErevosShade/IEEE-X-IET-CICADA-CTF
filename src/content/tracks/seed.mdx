## SEED — Grow Your Idea

SEED is the entry-level track for school students who want to tackle real-world challenges aligned with the **UN Sustainable Development Goals**.

This is where the next generation of engineers and technologists gets their first stage. Whether you're building a smart irrigation controller, a waste-sorting classifier, or a community health monitoring tool — SEED gives you the tools, mentors, and platform to make it real.

### What to Build

Choose any problem domain that maps to the SDGs:

- **SDG 3** — Good Health and Well-being
- **SDG 6** — Clean Water and Sanitation
- **SDG 7** — Affordable and Clean Energy
- **SDG 11** — Sustainable Cities and Communities
- **SDG 13** — Climate Action

### Team Composition

- **4 members** per team
- At least **one female member** is mandatory
- All members must be enrolled in Grade 9–12 at a recognised school

### What You'll Get

- Dedicated IEEE and IET mentor support throughout the sprint
- Access to hardware components and cloud compute credits
- Certificate of participation from IEEE × IET Chapters
- Winners featured on the CICADE Hall of Achievers
