## FRONTIER — Push the Limit

FRONTIER is for the builders who have shipped before and are ready for a harder problem. No guardrails, no scaffolded statements — just an open technical brief and 48 hours to build something that works at a level that could survive a production environment.

FRONTIER teams are expected to demonstrate technical depth, systems thinking, and an ability to make principled trade-offs under pressure.

### Technical Domains

Teams self-select into one of four technical domains:

- **Cybersecurity & Cryptography** — CTF-adjacent problem sets, exploit chains, hardened systems
- **AI & Machine Learning** — Adversarial robustness, edge inference, multimodal pipelines
- **Embedded & IoT** — Hardware-software co-design, sensor fusion, real-time systems
- **Distributed Systems** — Consensus protocols, fault tolerance, scalable architectures

### Judging Criteria

| Criterion | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Technical Depth | 35% |
| System Architecture | 25% |
| Demo Robustness | 20% |
| Innovation | 20% |
