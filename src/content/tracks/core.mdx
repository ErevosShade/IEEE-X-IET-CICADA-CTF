## CORE — Build Your First Real Thing

CORE is designed for first-time hackathon participants and emerging builders. You'll receive curated problem statements, guided workshops, and dedicated mentors — everything you need to ship something real within 36 hours.

This is not a beginner's consolation bracket. CORE is a full-fledged track with its own prizes, jury, and stage.

### Problem Domain

Teams receive a shortlist of **5 problem statements** 48 hours before the hackathon. You'll pick one and build a working prototype demonstrating:

- A clear understanding of the problem
- A technically sound solution approach
- A live demo or functional prototype
- A 3-minute pitch to the jury

### Judging Criteria

| Criterion | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Innovation & Creativity | 25% |
| Technical Implementation | 30% |
| Problem–Solution Fit | 25% |
| Presentation & Demo | 20% |

### What You'll Get

- IEEE × IET CORE Track winner certificate and trophy
- ₹20,000 prize pool across top 3 teams
- Internship fast-tracks with sponsoring organisations
- LinkedIn recommendation from chapter faculty advisors
