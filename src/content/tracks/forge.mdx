## FORGE — Industry Grade

FORGE is invitation-only, reserved for the highest-performing teams from previous CICADE editions and exceptional candidates identified through the FRONTIER screening process.

FORGE problems are co-authored with industry partners and represent **actual unsolved engineering challenges** from their product pipelines. Solutions with demonstrated viability may be taken forward as funded projects.

### What Makes FORGE Different

- Problems are **NDA-protected** until the sprint begins
- Mentors are **embedded engineers** from partner organisations, not student volunteers
- Judging is done by a **technical panel** that includes CTOs and senior engineers
- Solutions are evaluated for **production viability**, not just demo quality

### Eligibility

Invitations are extended to:

1. Top 3 teams from the previous CICADE FRONTIER track
2. Teams specifically nominated by FORGE partner organisations
3. Exceptional individuals identified during the CORE/FRONTIER screening process (may be re-teamed)

If you believe you qualify and have not received an invitation, contact us at forge@cicade.thapar.edu.

### Prizes

- **₹2,00,000** total prize pool
- FORGE Medallion — exclusive to FORGE winners
- Funded prototyping grants from industry partners
- Full-time and internship offers from participating organisations
